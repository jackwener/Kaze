      Questions

      Before you download.

      Something else? Ask on GitHub.

      Is Kaze free?

      Yes. Kaze is free and needs no account. The agent runs on a model you choose: sign in with ChatGPT, use your own API key, or point it at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

      Which computers does it run on?

      Macs on macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel; and Windows 10 or 11 PCs, x64 and ARM, from version 0.27.

      Does what I browse leave my computer?

      Your activity, history and settings stay on your computer. When you ask the agent for something, what it reads for that task goes to the model you picked, and nowhere else.

      Will the agent do things I didn't ask for?

      It works in tabs of its own and shows each step. Before it pays, sends, deletes or runs anything, it asks. Press ⌘. to take over at any moment.

      Do my Chrome extensions work?

      Yes. Bring them over from Chrome, or add new ones from the Chrome Web Store.

      How does it update?

      Kaze checks for new versions and updates itself. Every release and its notes are on GitHub.
